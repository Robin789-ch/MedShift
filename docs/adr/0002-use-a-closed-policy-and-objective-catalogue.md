# Use a closed policy and objective catalogue

V0.2 represents agent-editable scheduling decisions as a closed typed catalogue rather than a generic rule language. The scheduling policies are consecutive-shift limits, weekly shift-count limits, and forbidden shift transitions; the optimization objectives are consecutive-shift preferences, weekly shift-count preferences, shift transition preferences, employee preferences, workload balance, night-shift balance, remaining overtime, maximum remaining overtime, excess recovery, and consecutive department continuity. Department-specific run rules and department-to-department transition rules are deliberately deferred to a later version. Requests outside this catalogue are rejected explicitly rather than approximated, trading extensibility for predictable validation, solver translation, auditability, and safer agent behavior.
